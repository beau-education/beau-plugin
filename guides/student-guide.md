# Student Guide

Welcome to the learning platform. This guide explains how to navigate your dashboard, access your courses, and track your progress.

## Your Dashboard

### Setup

A **Setup** tile sits at the top of your home page. Each step has a **Go** button, and the tile tells you why the next one matters:

1. **Complete student profile**: needed before the first lesson. The student's age, interests and goals help each lesson start at the right level. It also asks about **allergies and medical needs**: choose **None**, or give the details. Only the student's teachers and your organisation's admins see the answer; it is never shared with the AI tutor.
2. **Verify your email**: needed before the first AI lesson. **Send the email again** is on that page if it hasn't arrived.
3. **Give consent for AI lessons** (if your organisation asks for it): needed before the first AI lesson. You can see what you agreed to, or withdraw it, under **Account › Parent or guardian**.
4. **Complete parent profile**: your name and postal address, for bookings and receipts (and, if your organisation records lessons, your answer on recording: Yes or No).
5. **Add lessons to calendar** (with in-person lessons only).

Once every step is done, the tile says **All set!**. Its guides and help stay underneath.

If the student or parent profile isn't finished when you press **Play** on a lesson, a short **Before the lesson** note says what's missing. Choose **Complete now**, or **Later, start the lesson** to carry on: it never stops a lesson, and asks only once per visit.

When you log in, you'll see your personalized learning dashboard with several sections:

### Your Homework

If your teacher has set you homework, it's at the top of your dashboard under **Your homework**. Each lesson shows:
- **Overdue by N days** (red) — the due date has passed, so do these first
- **Due today**, **Due tomorrow** or **Due** with the date — amber when it's within two days
- **Done** with your score, once you've finished it

Press **Start** to open your homework in the lesson player. The lesson list there shows the same **Due** and **Overdue** labels. When everything is done, the card says so — great work!

### Overall Progress

At the top, you'll see a summary card showing:
- Your completion percentage across all enrolled courses
- The number of resources you've completed out of the total
- How many courses you're enrolled in

### Your courses

**Your courses** shows the courses you're enrolled in. For each course, you can see:
- Course name
- Your progress bar
- Enrollment status (Active, Paused, or Completed)
- When you started the course

Click on a course row to expand it and see details about each resource in that course.

### Join a course

**Join a course**, near the top of your home page, lists the courses you can join or book yourself, without waiting for a teacher to invite you. Once you've joined one, it moves to **Your courses**.

For each available course, you can see:
- Course name and description (click "Read more" for long descriptions)
- Price (displayed with proper currency symbol like £, $, €) or "Free" badge
- Tags indicating the subject area
- Number of resources in the course
- Enrollment capacity (if limited)
- The teacher who will support you

#### Enrolling in Free Courses

1. Browse the courses under **Join a course**
2. Click **Enroll Now** on a course you're interested in
3. The course immediately appears in your **Your courses** section
4. Click **Play** to start learning

#### Purchasing Paid Courses

Some courses require payment:

1. Browse courses - paid courses display their price (e.g., £29.99, $19.99)
2. Click **Purchase Course** to proceed
3. Complete payment on the secure Stripe checkout page, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Pay by Bank (straight from your bank app)
4. After payment, you're automatically enrolled
5. The course appears in your **Your courses** section

**Note:** Some courses have enrollment limits. If a course shows "Course Full," you'll need to wait for a spot to open or contact your administrator.

### Resource Details

When you expand a course, you'll see each resource with:
- **Status**: Available, In Progress, Completed, or Locked
- **Attempts**: How many times you've attempted this resource
- **Score**: Your most recent score (if applicable)
- **Completed**: When you finished the resource

### Progress Charts

Your dashboard includes visual charts showing:
- Progress comparison across your enrolled courses
- A log of your recent activity

## In-Person Lessons

Some organisations also offer **in-person lessons** with a teacher, bought a half term at a time. You'll find them under **Join a course** on your home page (and on your organisation's homepage), marked **In person**.

### Booking

Click **Book lessons** on the course. You can book straight after creating your account: verifying your email and the consent form can wait until before the student's first AI lesson.

1. If we don't know yet, tell us who the lessons are for: the student's name and date of birth. We show the school year it means.
2. Choose the half term.
3. Choose a teacher, if there's more than one. You'll see their photo, subjects and prices.
4. Choose the lesson length and how many lessons a week.
5. Tell the teacher when suits you, for example "weekday afternoons after 4pm, not Wednesdays".
6. Pay for the half term at checkout, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Pay by Bank. Apple Pay shows in Safari or on an iPhone or iPad; Google Pay in Chrome or on Android with a card saved to Google.

The price covers every teaching week of the half term, whenever you book. A half term is on sale until its last week starts.

If you leave the checkout page without paying, just book again: your earlier checkout is cancelled, so you can't pay twice. With Pay by Bank, the booking is confirmed as soon as your bank sends the money.

### After you book

Your teacher gets in touch to agree lesson times with you. Until they're set, **My lessons** says "Booked: your teacher will be in touch to arrange lesson times".

Once the teacher has scheduled your lessons:

- You get an **email with every date**, and a calendar file to add them all at once.
- The next fortnight's lessons appear in **Your lessons** on your home page.
- Every lesson is on the calendar in **My lessons** (tap **All lessons** on your home page).

### My lessons

**My lessons** shows the half term's lessons on a calendar. Times are in your organisation's time zone.

Tap a lesson to see:

- the date, time and teacher, and where it is;
- its status: **Scheduled**, **Attended**, **Cancelled**, **Late cancellation** or **No-show**;
- how much notice the teacher needs to change it, and the date to tell them by;
- a **note from the teacher**, when they've sent one after the lesson (you also get it by email);
- **Download .ics**, to add that one lesson to your calendar.

### Lessons in your own calendar

On **My lessons**, under **Add lessons to your calendar**, tap **Make my calendar link**. Then **Open in my calendar app** (on an iPhone, this adds it to Calendar) or **Add to Google Calendar**. Your lessons appear there and stay up to date when a teacher moves one.

Keep the link private: anyone with it can see your lesson times. If you share it by mistake, **Make a new link** (the old one stops working), or **Turn off** the link altogether.

### Notes from your teacher

After a lesson, the teacher may send you a note: what you covered, what went well and something to practise at home. You get it by email, and the latest notes are on your home page under **Notes from your teacher**. **All notes** (on **My lessons**, below the calendar) lists every one.

### Lesson recordings

If your organisation records in-person lessons, **Account › Parent or guardian** asks whether the student's lessons may be recorded. With your yes, the teacher can record a lesson so that AI writes up notes on it for the teacher and for you. The recording itself is not kept, only a written transcript that only the teachers and the organisation's admins see. Saying no changes nothing else, and you can change your mind at any time.

### If you can't make a lesson

Message your teacher before their notice period ends (each lesson shows the date). If they cancel a lesson, it's owed to you and they'll arrange a new time.

### AI lessons with an in-person course

Some in-person courses come with **AI lessons** too, for practice between visits. They appear under your courses on your home page like any other course: press **Play**. AI lessons need the **Setup** steps done first: a verified email, and consent if your organisation asks for it.

## Accessing Courses

### Playing a Course

To start learning:
1. Find a course you're enrolled in on your dashboard
2. Click the **Play** button on the course
3. The player will open in a new browser tab
4. The bot will guide you through the available resources in the course

The course's progression type determines which resources are available and in what order.

### Delivery Modes

Resources can be delivered in three different modes:

**Conversation Mode**:
- You speak with the AI tutor using your microphone
- Have a two-way voice conversation
- Ask questions and get immediate responses
- The tutor adapts to your pace and understanding

**Presentation Mode**:
- The AI tutor narrates the content like a lecture
- No microphone needed
- Click "Next" to advance through sections
- Great for listening and learning at your own pace

**Worksheet Mode**:
- No tutor: you read each screen yourself and answer the questions, then press **Next**
- Your answers and score are saved, just like a lesson
- Prefer paper? Press **Print** at the top to print the worksheet
- **Narrated worksheets** are read aloud to you, one page at a time, like a slideshow with a voice — questions too. Use the round buttons to **pause/play** or **replay** a page, untick **Auto-play** if you'd rather press Next yourself, and use **Sound on/off** at the top to turn the voice off. **Back** returns to the previous page.

Beau's tutors, and the voice that reads narrated worksheets, are AI voices: made by a computer, not a real person. They can sometimes get things wrong, so if something doesn't sound right, ask your teacher.

### Resource Progress

As you work through a course, each resource tracks your progress:
- **Available**: Ready for you to work on
- **In Progress**: You've started but not completed
- **Completed**: You've finished this resource
- **Locked**: Not yet available (complete earlier resources first in sequential courses)

## Understanding Course Progression

Courses can have different progression types:

- **Flexible**: Complete resources in any order you prefer
- **Sequential**: Resources must be completed in a specific order
- **Random**: The system selects which resource is available next

Your teacher sets the progression type when creating the course.

## Taking Quizzes

During lessons, the AI tutor may present quizzes to check your understanding. There are four types of quizzes:

### Choice Questions (Single or Multiple)

When you see answer options:
1. **Read the question** carefully
2. **Select your answer(s)** - some quizzes allow only one answer, others allow multiple
3. **Click Submit** to check your answer
4. The tutor will let you know if you're correct

### Open Answer Questions

Some questions ask you to type your answer:
1. **Read the question** carefully
2. **Type your answer** in the text box
   - Some questions only accept numbers (integers or decimals)
   - Others accept any text
3. **Click Submit** to check your answer
4. The AI evaluates your response and provides feedback

### Ordered List Questions

Some questions ask you to arrange items in the correct order:
1. **Read the question** carefully (e.g., "Put these in order from smallest to largest")
2. **Drag and drop** the items to rearrange them into the correct sequence
3. **Click Submit** to check your order
4. You get credit for each item in the correct position

### Spoken Answer Questions

Some questions ask you to **say** your answer instead of typing it. There are two ways this looks, depending on the lesson.

**If you're talking with the tutor** (you can hear it and it can hear you), there's nothing to press — **just say your answer out loud**, the same way you've been talking all lesson. The tutor goes quiet and waits, so take your time.

The words we heard then appear on screen:

- **Send answer** — if that's what you meant
- **Say it again** — if it came out wrong, or we misheard you

If you don't choose, it sends your answer on its own after a few seconds.

**If the tutor is presenting to you** (it talks, you listen), you'll see a **Record** button instead:

1. **Press Record** and say your answer out loud (up to 2 minutes)
2. **Press Stop** when you're done
3. **Press Listen** to hear your answer back
4. **Press Record again** if you want another go — as many times as you like
5. **Click Submit** when you're happy with it

The first time you record, your browser will ask permission to use your microphone — choose **Allow**.

**In both cases**, the words we heard usually stay on screen long enough to read while the tutor talks about your answer. If they don't match what you meant to say, the microphone misheard you — say it again or record again, and don't worry, that's not a wrong answer.

Sometimes the words are **hidden on purpose** — you'll see "Answer recorded" instead. That happens when the next question asks you to *type* the same thing, so showing it would give you the answer. Your answer was still heard and marked.

Your answer is marked on **what you said**, not how you said it — so don't worry about "ums", pauses, or starting a sentence over. You can play your recording back later from your activity log, and your teacher can hear it too.

### Retry Attempts

If you answer incorrectly, the tutor may give you another chance:
- The tutor might provide a hint to help you
- Try a different answer and submit again
- Some quizzes limit the number of attempts

### Quiz Tips

- **Take your time** - read all the options before selecting or typing
- **Listen to hints** - the tutor's guidance can help you find the right answer
- **Don't worry about mistakes** - quizzes are for learning, not just testing
- **For open answer**: The AI understands different phrasings, so focus on the concept
- **For spoken answers**: Speak clearly and take your time — you can always listen back and record again before submitting

## Tracking Your Progress

### Scores

When you complete a resource, your score is recorded. Scores are displayed with color indicators:
- Green indicates strong performance
- Yellow indicates moderate performance
- Red indicates areas that may need review

### Activity Log

Your dashboard shows a chronological record of your completed resources, including:
- Course name
- Resource name
- Score achieved
- Completion date

## Getting Help

### Contacting Your Teacher

If you have questions about course content or need assistance, you can contact your teacher directly from the dashboard:

1. Find the course you have a question about in your **Your courses** section
2. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋮) on the right side of that course row
3. Select **Contact Teacher**
4. Fill in the subject and your message
5. Click **Send Message**

Your message will be sent directly to your assigned teacher for that course. They can see your progress and will respond to help you.

### Other Support

If signing in to a lesson doesn't work, you'll see **We couldn't sign you in**, with the reason when there is one. Check your email address and password, then press **Sign in again**.

If you're experiencing other technical issues with the platform (pages not loading, etc.), contact your administrator for assistance.
